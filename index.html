About us

Small team. Thoughtful ideas. Useful technology.

TWES LABS is a Uganda-based software development business focused on creating practical, user-friendly mobile applications and digital products.

Led by software developer Job Tumwesigye, we work as a small, collaborative team, bringing together development and UI/UX expertise as projects require. Our approach combines independent product development with collaboration to turn ideas into useful digital experiences.

We believe good technology should be approachable, purposeful and designed around the people who use it.

Practical by design We focus on real user needs.

Our approach

From a useful idea to a thoughtful product.
